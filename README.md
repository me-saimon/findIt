## How FindIt Works
##### 1. Report an Item
Post details about what you lost or found, including date, location, category, and an optional secret verification detail.

##### 2. Smart Matching
Our weighted algorithm pairs lost reports with found listings based on category, date proximity, location, and keywords.

##### 3. Verified Claim
True owners confirm identity by answering the secret question or providing proof of ownership before items are returned.
