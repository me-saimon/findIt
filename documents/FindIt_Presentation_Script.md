# FindIt — 5-Minute Presentation Script
### ~28–30 seconds per slide (10 slides, ~5 minutes total)

---

## Slide 1 — Title
**"Good [morning/afternoon] everyone. I'm [Your Name], and with my teammate [Partner's Name], we're presenting FindIt — a centralized, smart Lost & Found Management System built on ASP.NET Core MVC, for CSE 4106, Software Development Lab-1."**

---

## Slide 2 — Project Abstract
**"Losing a wallet, phone, or ID card is common in busy places like universities and offices. Today, recovery relies on scattered social posts and notice boards, which rarely work. FindIt centralizes this into one platform where users report, search, message, and claim items — powered by a smart matching engine that automatically suggests lost-found pairs by category, location, and date."**

---

## Slide 3 — Problem Statement
**"Four core problems drive this need: Fragmentation — info scattered across platforms; Low Visibility — posts get buried fast; Lack of Verification — no secure way to confirm ownership; and Inefficiency — no automated way to match reports. FindIt is built to close all four gaps."**

---

## Slide 4 — Objectives
**"Our four objectives: build a centralized ASP.NET Core MVC platform; implement a weighted smart-matching algorithm; protect user trust through secure messaging and claim verification; and deliver a fast, mobile-responsive experience that gets belongings back quickly."**

---

## Slide 5 — Scope of the Project
**"In-scope for version one: role-based web app, secure login, item reporting with image uploads, the matching engine, a verified claiming workflow, and an admin dashboard. Out of scope for now: native mobile apps, IoT trackers, and payment systems — deliberate trade-offs to keep our first release focused."**

---

## Slide 6 — Methodology
**"We're using Agile development on an MVC architecture: ASP.NET Core with C# on the backend, SQL Server via Entity Framework Core for the database, and Razor Views with Bootstrap 5 and AJAX on the frontend. A weighted scoring algorithm powers the matching logic on every new submission."**

---

## Slide 7 — Project Timeline
**"Our plan spans 10 weeks — starting with requirements and design, moving through authentication, the reporting module, and search in the middle weeks, building the smart matching algorithm around weeks six and seven, then closing with claims, the admin dashboard, and final testing and deployment."**

---

## Slide 8 — Budget
**"Every tool in our stack — Visual Studio, SQL Server Express, Git, Bootstrap, Leaflet.js, Chart.js — is free and open-source. That puts our total development cost at zero dollars, with no licensing or hosting risk."**

---

## Slide 9 — Risk Analysis
**"We've planned for four key risks: low adoption, mitigated with an intuitive UI and campus partnerships; false claims, prevented through hidden-identifier verification; privacy breaches, addressed with encryption and in-app messaging; and performance issues, handled through query optimization and async processing."**

---

## Slide 10 — Conclusion
**"FindIt replaces scattered, manual recovery methods with one centralized, intelligent platform. Its smart matching and secure verification make it efficient and trustworthy — with real potential to become an essential utility for universities and offices beyond our campus. Thank you — we're happy to take any questions."**

---

### Delivery tips
- **Pace:** ~28–30 seconds per slide, spoken at a natural, slightly brisk pace — rehearse with a timer.
- **Cut on the fly:** If you're running long, Slides 6 and 8 (Methodology, Budget) compress easiest — drop to one sentence each.
- **Don't read verbatim:** Internalize the one key idea per slide rather than reciting word-for-word; it'll sound more natural and keep you closer to time.
- **Q&A prep:** Be ready to expand on the matching algorithm's weighting and the claim-verification process — the two most likely follow-up questions.
